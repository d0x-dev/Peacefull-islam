Privacy Policy for Peaceful Islam

Last updated: October 1, 2026

Welcome to Peaceful Islam. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our app handles your information.

1. Information We Collect

Peaceful Islam is designed with privacy at its core:

  • Location Data: If granted, approximate or precise location is used locally on your device solely to calculate accurate Islamic prayer times and Qibla compass direction. We do not store or transmit your location to any remote servers.
  • Audio & Media Playback: The app streams and plays Quran recitations. Media playback state and foreground service notifications run locally to provide uninterrupted listening.
  • No Personal Data Collection: We do not require account registration, nor do we collect your name, phone number, email address, or contacts.

2. Third-Party Services

The app accesses public Islamic APIs and CDNs solely to fetch Quran surahs, ayahs, translations, and hadith collections. No personally identifiable information (PII) is shared with these services.

3. Data Security

We do not store your personal information on external servers. All preferences (such as prayer calculation methods and last read surah) remain stored locally on your device.
